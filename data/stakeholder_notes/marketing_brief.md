# Marketing brief: how we report the funnel

From: Regional Marketing Director, UK & Ireland
To: BI Manager; cc Digital Acquisition Lead
Re: Q2 channel reporting - definitions for the new dashboard

I know the platform team is rebuilding the marketing-to-sales reporting this
quarter, and I want my numbers on record before anyone bakes in a definition
I haven't agreed to. My team is accountable for channel budget, and the
board pack goes out on my numbers, so here is how we calculate the two
figures that matter to me.

**Cost Per Lead.** This is channel spend divided by all leads captured. All
of them - I don't want leads quietly dropped because someone decided two
records looked similar, and I don't want a consent filter sitting in front
of the calculation. If a lead came in against a campaign, it counts. My
team is judged on cost efficiency at the point of capture, not on what a
downstream system decides to keep.

**Conversion.** This is policies divided by leads captured. Same
denominator as Cost Per Lead, for consistency - one number, one lead count,
used everywhere I report channel performance.

**Attribution.** The lead is attributed to the campaign that is recorded on
it at creation. First touch. That's the campaign we paid for and the
campaign that should get the credit, regardless of what happens to the lead
afterwards.

**Reporting period.** The reporting month is the month the lead was
created. My budget is spent in the month the lead lands, so that's the
month the performance has to land in too.

I'd rather agree this once than have it re-litigated every time someone
runs a different query. Happy to talk it through, but these four points are
not up for silent reinterpretation by whoever builds the model.
